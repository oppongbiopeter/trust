# PRODUCTION CERTIFICATION

## Gate 1 — Corporate/legal
- legal entity structure approved
- lender/funder/servicer responsibilities documented
- contracts executed
- IP ownership established
- insurance evaluated
- tax structure reviewed.

## Gate 2 — Regulatory
- correct BoG authorization/licence confirmed
- DPC registration/status confirmed
- DPIA approved where required
- AML/compliance framework approved
- credit reporting readiness confirmed
- consumer disclosures approved.

## Gate 3 — Product
- products approved
- pricing approved
- affordability methodology approved
- collections policy approved
- complaints process approved.

## Gate 4 — Engineering
- API contract locked
- DB migrations tested
- event registry locked
- observability operational
- performance tested
- security scans passed.

## Gate 5 — Financial
- chart of accounts approved
- ledger certified
- accounting mappings certified
- treasury controls certified
- payment providers certified
- reconciliation certified.

## Gate 6 — Risk
- credit model validated
- fraud controls tested
- AML monitoring tested
- exposure limits tested
- overrides tested
- kill switches tested.

## Gate 7 — Security
- threat model completed
- penetration test completed
- red-team exercise completed
- secrets review
- IAM review
- backup/restore
- ransomware exercise.

## Gate 8 — Operations
- support staffed
- collections staffed
- incident rota
- runbooks
- escalation matrix
- provider contacts
- complaints handling.

## Gate 9 — Pilot
- synthetic/internal test
- controlled customer cohort
- enhanced manual review
- daily reconciliation
- daily risk review
- explicit stop criteria.

## Stop conditions

Immediately pause affected workflow for:
- unexplained ledger imbalance
- duplicate disbursement
- material reconciliation break
- payment-state corruption
- critical identity failure
- serious data leak
- uncontrolled privileged access
- model/policy version mismatch
- material regulatory breach
- unexplained portfolio anomaly.
