# FINAL DEFINITION OF DONE

Trust is not production-ready until all applicable items are true.

## Business
- legal entity and lender role clear
- funding model clear
- partner/vendor responsibilities clear.

## Regulatory
- required authorization/licensing complete
- consumer disclosures approved
- DPC requirements addressed
- AML framework operational
- credit reporting obligations addressed
- complaint process operational.
